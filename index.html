How to play Big Bang Boggle

1

Join

Click the link in the Teams chat. It's best on your laptop. Enter your name and wait for the countdown. (Phones work too: scan the QR code.)

2

Make words

Join letters that touch: up, down, sideways or diagonally. Each letter can only be used once per word. Words need 3+ letters.

3

Enter them fast

On a laptop, just type the word and press Enter. Or drag across the letters with your mouse or finger.

4

Score

3 letters 1 pt4 letters 2 pts5 letters 3 pts6 letters 4 pts…and so on
5

Post your score

When time's up, screenshot your score and post it in the chat. Highest score wins the round. We'll play 3 rounds!

Example: T → E → A → M makes TEAM = 2 pts.
A → M goes diagonally, which is allowed.
